About Glastoma AI

Everything you need to know about the model, the data, and the safeguards.

How it works

A deep-learning ensemble trained on the HAM10000 dataset classifies dermoscopic images into 7 clinically significant categories. Inference is performed in your browser, with no images leaving the device.

Privacy & security

All images are blurred on-screen by default and never persisted. The application does not transmit images to any server. You decide what to save, and you can wipe history at any time.

Medical disclaimer

Glastoma AI is intended for educational and informational purposes only. It does not replace professional medical evaluation. Always consult a qualified dermatologist for diagnosis and treatment.

Technology

Built with TensorFlow.js for client-side inference, PWA standards for offline use, and a privacy-first architecture. The interface is fully bilingual and respects RTL conventions.
